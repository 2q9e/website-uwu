# Media provenance and permissions

Reviewed November 19, 2026. These records document inputs to the site build and are kept outside the published pages. The copies in the excluded reference folder were used for visual comparison. The assets in `site/assets/` were retrieved separately from their public sources.

| Asset | Origin and contribution | Processing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `regionals.webp` | The lead photograph in DeGroote School of Business (2025) shows the Enactus McMaster group at the 2025 regional competition. Public access establishes provenance. | The 800 × 533 source was resampled to 1600 × 1066 with Lanczos and lightly sharpened. |
| `regionals-hd.webp` | The same public 800 × 533 lead image is served on the original post and on DeGroote's reshare (Trifunovic, 2025). | A 2400 × 1599 Lanczos resample with light sharpening. |
| `nationals.webp` | The lead photograph in Siddique (2025) shows the Enactus McMaster group at the National Exposition in Calgary. Public access establishes provenance. | The 800 × 533 source was resampled to 1600 × 1066 with Lanczos and lightly sharpened. |
| `nationals-hd.webp` | The same public 800 × 533 National Exposition photo (Siddique, 2025). | A 2400 × 1599 Lanczos resample with light sharpening. |
| `campus.webp` | The Office of the President's contact page includes a [matching higher resolution image of University Hall](https://president.mcmaster.ca/app/uploads/2020/08/univeristyhallSpring2020-2_jpg.jpg) (McMaster University, n.d.). | The 1800 × 1201 source was cropped to the reference's 16:9 composition and encoded at 1600 × 900. |
| `campus-hd.webp` | The same public 1800 × 1201 University Hall photo (McMaster University, n.d.). | The 1800 × 1012 crop uses the full published source width without enlarging it. |
| `favicon.png` | The [original 300 × 300 Enactus Canada origami favicon](https://enactus.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Enactus_favicon-300x300.png) comes from the Enactus Canada website. The brand guide identifies this official mark and describes its usage (Enactus Canada, n.d.). | PNG re-encoding preserves the mark and its transparency. |
| `enactus-mcmaster.png` | The chapter's [profile logo](https://ugc.production.linktr.ee/3e317726-027b-4b86-9342-ad9433b8dc3c_EnactusOrigamiMcMasterUniLOGO--4-.png?io=true&size=avatar-v3_0) comes from its public Linktree (Enactus McMaster, n.d.). | The profile image was decoded and saved as a 357 × 357 PNG without visual changes. |
| `card.jpg` | An original 1200 × 630 social card composition uses the regional team photograph and the Enactus origami mark above. | The photo stays within its original aspect ratio. The composition adds the site title and description. |
| `fonts/*.woff2` | Google's Latin webfont distributions of [Barlow Condensed](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Barlow+Condensed) at 600/700 and [DM Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Sans) at 400–700 supply the typography used by the reference. | Webfonts are self-hosted and used unmodified. Their upstream SIL Open Font License 1.1 notices accompany the font files. |

The high density team images are upscales of the verified public files. Their captured detail remains limited to the source photos. All photographs and marks retain the rights of their respective owners. The source viewing license covers only original material to which the project can grant rights. The public photograph posts and the campus page do not state a redistribution license. Use of the Enactus marks is governed by the brand owner. The operator should confirm publication permissions with the respective owners, including permission for the regional photograph's use in the share card. The two OFL texts in `site/assets/fonts/` carry the applicable licensing terms for their fonts.
