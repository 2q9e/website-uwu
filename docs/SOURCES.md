## Editorial limits

- Use the chapter's public messaging channels for general or privacy inquiries until the chapter supplies a role-based mailbox. A direct chapter mailbox and a current student union listing were not verified.
- Use dated team roles. A LinkedIn affiliation does not establish a current leadership appointment. A participant's account can support their attendance and role without supporting other attendees' exact titles or a current roster.
- The reference's LinkedIn posts establish historical context for regional and national event photos. Their public visibility does not establish reuse rights. Credit licensed photography by the terms of its own license. The source-available license for original website materials does not supersede third-party rights.
- The privacy notice should cover the site's actual features and clarify when a visitor leaves for Linktree, a social network, or a recruitment form. Host log access and retention, applicant handling, and a designated privacy mailbox have not been verified. The Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (2019) explains that PIPEDA applicability depends on the activity, so the website need not declare exemption or compliance. Source terms should preserve any applicable statutory exceptions (Copyright Act, 1985, ss. 29–29.2).
- The source-available license should preserve GitHub's existing public repository permissions, which include in-service viewing and forking. Those permissions do not themselves grant additional rights to use code outside GitHub's service (GitHub, 2026, § D.5).
